Security Tips

🔐

Use unique passwords for different accounts

Never reuse the same password across multiple sites. If one account is compromised, all your other accounts remain secure.

🆙

Enable two-factor authentication

Add an extra layer of security to your accounts. Even if someone steals your password, they can't access your account without the second factor.

🚫

Avoid suspicious links

Don't click on links in unexpected emails or messages. Hover over links to see the actual destination before clicking.

📱

Keep software updated

Regularly update your operating system, apps, and antivirus software. Updates often include critical security patches.

🙅

Never share sensitive information with strangers

Be wary of unsolicited requests for personal information, whether by email, phone, or message.

🌐

Check website URLs before entering personal information

Verify you're on the legitimate website before entering passwords or financial details. Look for HTTPS and the correct domain name.

Phishing

Phishing is a cyber attack that tricks you into giving up sensitive information like passwords or credit card numbers by pretending to be a trustworthy entity. Common signs include suspicious sender addresses, urgent language, and unexpected attachments.

How to protect yourself:

  • Verify sender addresses before clicking links
  • Look for spelling and grammar mistakes
  • When in doubt, contact the organization directly
×

Password Security

Strong password security is your first line of defense online. Use unique, complex passwords for each account and consider using a password manager to keep track of them securely.

Best practices:

  • Use at least 12 characters with mixed types
  • Never reuse passwords across important accounts
  • Enable two-factor authentication wherever possible
×

Malware

Malware (malicious software) includes viruses, ransomware, spyware, and trojans that can infiltrate your devices. It often spreads through suspicious downloads, email attachments, or compromised websites.

Prevention tips:

  • Keep your software and antivirus updated
  • Avoid downloading from untrusted sources
  • Be cautious with email attachments
×

Social Engineering

Social engineering exploits human psychology rather than technical vulnerabilities. Attackers manipulate people into breaking normal security procedures. Common tactics include pretexting, baiting, and tailgating.

Stay safe by:

  • Verifying identities before sharing information
  • Being skeptical of unsolicited requests
  • Educating yourself and others regularly
×

Online Privacy

Protecting your online privacy involves managing what personal information you share and who can access it. With proper privacy practices, you can reduce your digital footprint and minimize targeted advertising and profiling.

Key steps:

  • Review and adjust social media privacy settings
  • Use browser extensions that block trackers
  • Be mindful of what you share publicly
×

Safe Browsing

Safe browsing practices help you avoid malicious websites, downloads, and online threats. Always check that websites use HTTPS and be cautious of unexpected pop-ups or warnings.

Tips for secure browsing:

  • Look for the padlock icon and HTTPS in the address bar
  • Avoid clicking on suspicious pop-ups
  • Use a reputable ad-blocker
